Tube Furnace

Design complete

Lead: Andrew Choi

What It Does

The tube furnace is a kanthal-element resistance furnace designed to reach 1100 °C for annealing and oxidation of doped silicon wafers.

The heating element is a 22 AWG Kanthal A1 coil of 24 turns, doubled, with a design resistance of about 18.46 Ω, drawing roughly 780 W at 120 V. Temperature is held by a PID controller driving a zero-cross solid-state relay in time-proportioning mode, with an independent high-limit thermal cutoff as a separate protective layer. The wound zone is cemented and wrapped in three layers of ceramic wool, roughly 76 mm in total, inside a sheet-metal casing; a thermocouple probe enters radially at the coil midpoint.

Thermal and electrical design, component selection, and the calculations behind them are complete. The build is estimated at about four weeks, most of that spent waiting on the custom quartz tube rather than on assembly.
