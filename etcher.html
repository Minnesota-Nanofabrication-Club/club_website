Reactive-Ion Etcher

In progress

The etcher's own status document puts the work at cleaning up the design and acquiring components. The rest of the folder holds a plan — a project timeline and an architecture diagram — rather than a record of build progress.

What It Does

The etcher is the etching tool of the club's fabrication line. The system to be built is a vacuum chamber with a regulated gas intake and exhaust system, its own gas-flow tubing and electrical system, and the measuring and regulating equipment needed to control a process run.

The plan brings the machine up in two steps: first working the system primarily without toxic gases, then bringing those process gases in for final testing. By the end of the semester the club expects a fully functioning prototype with a regulated gas intake and exhaust system and complete build and operating documentation.

Project Timeline

The plan runs over a semester, in the stages below.
